A little context.

The positions need somewhere to land. The processes are the important part, and plain is better than tidy: they are what the ceremony audit works on.

No layoff plans. The Kurultai does not produce headcount-reduction recommendations, ever. Moving the staffing slider never removes that commitment.

What travels where. What you type is sent to Anthropic's API to be read, and comes back as your document. Nothing is stored on our side but a count, so the day's limit can be enforced, and the finished document, which deletes itself within two hours. No account, no tracking, no record you did not take with you.

The mind behind the name.

We chose a strategist as our patron figure. Not a conqueror on a horse.

Sorghaghtani Beki cultivated political alliances, supported education, and helped bring Möngke to power in 1251, as described in Timothy May's biography for EBSCO.

Our reading is a design principle: build the conditions for sound decisions. Leave the mythology out. Named for Sorghaghtani Beki, the strategist who never took the throne and raised four rulers. Succession, not dependency.
