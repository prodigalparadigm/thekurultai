The strategist.
Not the spectacle.

The Kurultai is an AI strategy tool built by Prodigal Paradigm; this page is about the strategist it is named for. Sorghaghtani Beki is the patron figure of The Kurultai. Our interest is in judgment, preparation, and the people who make capability possible.

Gather the council.

A quriltai was a council used to elect a khan, among the political events described in Timothy May's biography of Sorghaghtani Beki.

We take the name as an invitation to deliberate. Not to agree automatically, and not to mistake one confident voice for a strategy.

Build the capacity.

Sorghaghtani arranged education for her sons and supported Muslim religious institutions while also cultivating relationships with Buddhist and Daoist figures, according to May's account.

Our interpretation: strategy includes preparing people to act. A document that assumes away the people who must carry it out has not finished the work.

Make the alliance.

1251

Her political alliances helped secure the election of her son Möngke as khan in 1251, as described by May for EBSCO.

The modern analogy has limits. Our point is narrower: authority does not remove the need for preparation, relationships, and a workable decision.

Keep the receipts.

May cautions that important chroniclers served Sorghaghtani's descendants and that their favorable portrayals require scrutiny; he also describes the purge and later succession conflict surrounding her family's rise, in the same biography.

That is part of the story, not an inconvenient footnote. We are borrowing a commitment to deliberation, not endorsing empire or turning a complicated historical figure into a modern corporate saint.
