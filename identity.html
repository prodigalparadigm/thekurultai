The Kurultai

A place to assemble.

The proposed mark is four inward-facing bars around shared ground. It echoes four inputs and a council without claiming to be a Mongolian historical symbol.

Use the symbol by itself at favicon scale, or beside the serif wordmark. Preserve the open corners. The mark is newly drawn for this concept, not an adaptation of a clan sign, seal, or religious emblem.

Single-color use is permitted. At 16 pixels, simplify to the four bars and central square with no additional detail. Trademark and similarity clearance remain separate pre-launch work.

Ink, paper, decision.

Green establishes the family resemblance. Vermilion identifies the point of decision. Blue and ochre support evidence and context rather than competing for attention.

Use paper and ink for most of the interface. Vermilion belongs on the slider handle and active selection. No status relies on hue alone.

A family resemblance.

The existing Prodigal Paradigm homepage uses Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans alongside cream, green, and gold. This concept keeps the type family and gives the product a more assertive editorial scale.

Judgment,
in plain sight.

Cormorant Garamond 500 for display and voiced poles. DM Sans 400/500/600 for body copy, controls, evidence, and tables.

Body copy: 16-18 px on the web. Report text: 10 pt in print. The pole sentences get room before anything is compressed.

Two alternate emphases.

These are bounded variations for the revision loop, not three unrelated brands. The recommended working-paper direction stays the baseline.

The Horizon

Promote slate blue into the principal field color; keep ink-green text and ochre details. Quieter, more institutional, less visibly connected to the parent practice.

The Vermilion Seal

Use the vermilion mark more prominently and keep pages almost entirely paper and ink. Stronger editorial character; use sparingly so it does not become theatrical.

The instrument states.

The notebook's vertical scales remain vertical. On desktop they form two pairs; on a phone they stack in reading order. A screen reader receives the dimension, position, and both full sentences.

Exercise the working component

Rest & change

Center is neutral. Moving the handle updates a location number and a plain-language description. No preference is called “good” or “bad.”

Keyboard focus

A visible blue focus outline surrounds the native range. Arrow keys move by one; Page Up/Down by ten; Home/End select the endpoints.

Waiting & success

Four bars move during the explicitly labeled deliberation demo. Reduced-motion preferences stop the animation. Completion shows the intake summary, not a fabricated strategy.

Error & disabled

Missing intake fields receive text errors and focus moves to the first missing field. The submit control is disabled while the demo runs; the existing values remain intact.

Original image language.

The patron image is an imagined council scene rendered as a restrained editorial print. It is not a historical likeness, a surviving artifact, or a copy of the attached graphic biography.

Original AI-generated concept. No source artwork was passed as a generation reference. Period dress and scene are interpretive, not historically certified.
